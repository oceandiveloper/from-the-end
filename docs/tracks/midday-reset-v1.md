# Midday Track v1: "Reset" (when everything is too much)

Status: **v1, voiced right away** at Kevin's request. Change anything that feels wrong and it gets re-voiced.
Use: in the middle of the day, when the nervous system is flooded, eyes open or closed, sitting or standing. About 7 minutes. Ends awake, so you go back into the day.
Voice: ElevenLabs "James". `[pause Xs]` = silence added during stitching.
Source for the identity lines: `docs/kevin-2.0.md` (Anchored, Unbothered, Calm power, Breath-led, "Responds, doesn't react").

Rule check:
- Identity lines appear 2 times in the whole track.
- No plans, no amounts, no promise.
- No claimed feelings: the tension is named as weather, never denied, never fixed.

---

## 1. Stop

Stop for a moment.

[pause 3s]

Nothing needs you for the next few minutes. Whatever is waiting can wait. You can stay where you are, eyes open or closed.

[pause 5s]

---

## 2. Breath

Let's bring the body down first.

[pause 2s]

Breathe in through your nose… and then take one more short sip of air on top… and now let it all out through your mouth, long and slow.

[pause 8s]

Again. In through the nose… one more little sip on top… and a long, slow breath out.

[pause 8s]

One more time. In… sip… and let it go, all the way out.

[pause 10s]

Now just breathe normally, but let every breath out be a little longer than the breath in.

[pause 15s]

Feel your feet on the floor. Let your hands get heavy. Let your shoulders drop, even just a little.

[pause 15s]

Breath out longer than breath in. Nothing else to do.

[pause 20s]

---

## 3. Weather

There's a lot right now. You don't have to pretend there isn't.

[pause 5s]

Notice it the way you'd notice weather. The pressure. The heat in the chest. The racing thoughts. It's moving through. It is not who you are.

[pause 10s]

You don't have to fix it. You don't have to argue with it. You're the one watching it pass.

[pause 15s]

The weather is loud. The one watching it is quiet.

[pause 15s]

---

## 4. Anchor

Now come back to the one who is watching.

[pause 5s]

Feel the line of your spine, from the base all the way up to the back of your head. Like roots going down and a thread going up.

[pause 10s]

This is the place nothing can reach. It was here before this day started, and it will be here when the day is over.

[pause 10s]

I am anchored, unbothered and certain.

[pause 20s]

From here, nothing has to be rushed. Not the next email, not the next call, not the next decision. Each thing gets met when it arrives, one at a time.

[pause 15s]

I respond. I don't react.

[pause 20s]

Calm power. Not loud. Not forced. Just steady, like breath.

[pause 15s]

---

## 5. Return

Take one more slow breath in… and a long breath out.

[pause 6s]

Feel your feet again. Notice the room around you. The sounds. The light.

[pause 6s]

Whatever comes next, you meet it from here. You can go back now, at your own pace.

[pause 5s]

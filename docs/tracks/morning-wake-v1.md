# Morning Track v1: "Wake up in the end"

Status: **v1, voiced right away** so Kevin has it the next morning. Change anything that feels wrong and it gets re-voiced.
Use: right after waking, still lying down, **before the phone**. About 10 minutes. Ends awake, then you get up.
Voice: ElevenLabs "James". `[pause Xs]` = silence added during stitching.
Source: `docs/kevin-2.0.md`. Three Level-100 lines in Kevin's own words: statement 1 (without the amount), 6 and 9. Later versions rotate the three lines daily.

Rule check:
- Identity lines: three, each spoken once. The long silences after them are for Kevin to repeat them inwardly as often as he wants.
- No amounts (statement 1 without the 20,000 €), no plans, no promise.
- No claimed feelings: the voice invites ("let", "notice"), it never says "you feel".

---

## 1. Before

Good morning.

[pause 4s]

Don't reach for the phone yet. Stay right where you are. Eyes closed, or half open. The day can wait a few minutes.

[pause 8s]

You're still close to sleep. That's good. This is the softest moment of the day, before the old thoughts wake up.

[pause 10s]

Take one slow breath in… and let it out, long and easy.

[pause 10s]

---

## 2. Arrive

Notice the bed under you. The weight of the blanket. The air on your face.

[pause 10s]

The night is over, and you woke up as the one you already are. Not someone who's getting there. The one who's there.

[pause 12s]

Nothing needs to be figured out right now. Before you think about the day, you get to decide who's waking up into it.

[pause 15s]

---

## 3. Three lines

Here are three of your own lines. Let each one come to you slowly. After each one, there's silence. Say it inwardly, as many times as you like, until it sounds like something you simply know.

[pause 8s]

I generate money with freedom, creativity and purpose, with ease.

[pause 90s]

I live in vibrant health. Strong, disciplined, nourished and energized.

[pause 90s]

I move through life guided by intuition, grounded in trust, and anchored in spirit.

[pause 90s]

Let all three settle. You don't have to hold on to them. They're yours.

[pause 15s]

---

## 4. Carry it

Now pick one word to take with you into the day. Maybe it's calm power. Maybe anchored. Maybe still. Whatever comes first.

[pause 15s]

That's your word for today. Whenever the day gets loud, you come back to it. One breath, one word, and you're home again.

[pause 15s]

---

## 5. Rise

Take one more deep breath in… and let it out slowly.

[pause 6s]

Stretch a little, if you like. Feel your hands, your feet.

[pause 8s]

When you're ready, get up as the one you are. The day is already yours.

[pause 5s]

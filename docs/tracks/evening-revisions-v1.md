# Evening Revision Modules v1: Work, Family, Calm

Status: **v1, voiced** (2026-11-09). Pre-mixed as `tracks/evening-variants/evening-<name>-v1.mp3`, in the bot under `/evening`. Money exists in `evening-money-v1.md`.
Each module replaces section 2 of the evening track: **Landing → Revision (one of these) → Scene → Drift**. Landing, Scene and Drift stay the same.
Voice: ElevenLabs "James", slow and low. `[pause Xs]` = silence added during stitching.

Rule check (same as Money):
- Always "you", never "he" or "she": the old moment is seen from outside (like a film), the new version is lived from inside (Neville: think *from* the end, not *of* it). Gender-neutral, so the tracks work for anyone.
- No "I am" lines here. The three identity lines of the evening track all live in the Scene. The Revisions describe what you do at level one hundred, taken from the Standards in `docs/kevin-2.0.md`.
- No plans, no amounts, no promise that anything will work.
- No claimed feelings: the voice invites ("let", "notice", "step into it"), never "you feel".
- Family is about Kevin, not about anyone else being wrong. Nobody in the scene gets blamed or changed, only how you meet it.

## 2. Revision: Work (≈ 5 min, for days when work pulled you down)

Tonight, let one moment from today's work come back to you. Just one. Maybe a message from a client. Maybe a task that didn't move. Maybe the feeling of being behind.

[pause 10s]

Don't go back into it. Just see it, like a scene from a film. There it is.

[pause 8s]

Now let it play again. Same desk, same moment. But this time, step into it as the one you already are at level one hundred.

[pause 6s]

The pressure arrives, and you don't speed up. You take one breath before you answer. Your shoulders stay down.

[pause 10s]

You don't need to prove anything. Not to the client, not to yourself. You look at the problem, not at what it might say about you.

[pause 10s]

You work on the problem. You don't carry it around.

[pause 12s]

You do the next right thing, and then the next. When it's enough for the day, you let it be enough.

[pause 10s]

Let the scene play one more time, from the beginning, this way. Clear. Steady. In your own time.

[pause 25s]

That is how today went.

[pause 5s]

Let the old version go. It doesn't need to be kept. This one is the one you take with you into the night.

[pause 10s]

## 2. Revision: Family (≈ 5 min, for days when the pressure reached home)

Tonight, let one moment from home come back to you. Just one. Maybe you were short with someone. Maybe you weren't really there. Maybe the worry came through the door with you.

[pause 10s]

Don't judge it. Just see it, like a scene from a film. There it is.

[pause 8s]

Now let it play again. Same room, same moment. But this time, step into it as the one you already are at level one hundred.

[pause 6s]

You come through the door, and you leave the day outside. Whatever is open stays at the desk.

[pause 10s]

Someone needs you, and you're there. You kneel down. You listen. Your voice stays soft, even when it's loud around you.

[pause 10s]

You keep the chaos away from your family. Not by holding everything in, but because the chaos simply doesn't live in you.

[pause 12s]

Nobody has to be different for you to be calm. You are the quiet in the room.

[pause 10s]

Let the scene play one more time, from the beginning, this way. Present. Warm. Unhurried.

[pause 25s]

That is how today went.

[pause 5s]

Let the old version go. It doesn't need to be kept. This one is the one you take with you into the night.

[pause 10s]

## 2. Revision: Calm (≈ 4 min, for days with nothing to revise)

Tonight there's nothing to fix. Let one good moment from today come back to you instead. It doesn't have to be big. A coffee. A laugh. A minute where everything was simply fine.

[pause 12s]

There it is. Let it come closer.

[pause 8s]

Notice where you were. The light. The sounds around you. Who was there, or that you were alone.

[pause 12s]

That moment wasn't luck. That's what it looks like when you live from where you already are.

[pause 10s]

Let it play once more, slowly. Stay in it a little longer than it lasted today.

[pause 30s]

Let this be the day you keep.

[pause 10s]

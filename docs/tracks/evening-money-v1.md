# Evening Track v1: "Fall asleep in the end" (Money)

Status: **draft for Kevin to read**. No audio generated yet.
Voice: ElevenLabs "James", slow and low. `[pause Xs]` = silence that gets added during stitching. ElevenLabs only voices the text.
Source for the identity lines: `docs/kevin-2.0.md` (Level 100, Standards).

How the modules are assembled: **Landing → Revision (Money) → Scene → Drift**. Landing and Drift are the same every night. The Revision changes with the day (Work / Family / Money / Calm). The Scene stays the same for weeks.

Rule check:
- The identity lines appear 3 times in the whole track, all in the Scene.
- No amounts, no plans, no promise that it will work.
- No claimed feelings: the voice invites ("let", "notice") and never says "you feel calm".

---

## 1. Landing (≈ 3–4 min, the same every night)

You can let the day end now.

[pause 5s]

There is nothing left to do tonight. Whatever is still open will still be there tomorrow, and tomorrow is not now.

[pause 5s]

Let your body be heavy. Let the bed hold you. You don't have to hold yourself up anymore.

[pause 8s]

Take one slow breath in through your nose… and let it fall out, slowly.

[pause 6s]

Once more. In… and let it go.

[pause 8s]

Notice your jaw. If it's tight, let it soften. Let your tongue rest. Let your shoulders sink a little further.

[pause 8s]

Notice your hands. Let them be open. Nothing to hold. Nothing to grip.

[pause 10s]

Your breath doesn't need you anymore. It knows how to breathe on its own. Just let it.

[pause 15s]

The day is done.

[pause 10s]

---

## 2. Revision: Money (≈ 5 min, for days when money pulled you down)

Tonight, let one moment from today come back to you. Just one. The moment money pulled you into the old level. Maybe an email. Maybe a number on a screen. Maybe a question you didn't want to hear.

[pause 10s]

Don't relive it. Just see it, the way you'd see a scene from a film. There it is.

[pause 8s]

Now let it play again. Same place, same moment. But this time, step into it as the one you already are at level one hundred.

[pause 6s]

The message arrives, and you read it slowly. Your breath stays low. Your shoulders stay down. Nothing in you rushes.

[pause 10s]

You don't need to defend yourself. You don't need anyone to understand you. You simply know what is yours to do, and you do it in your own time.

[pause 10s]

You respond. You don't react.

[pause 12s]

Let the scene play one more time, from the beginning, this way. Calm. Clear. Untouched.

[pause 25s]

That is how today went.

[pause 5s]

Let the old version go. It doesn't need to be kept. This one is the one you take with you into the night.

[pause 10s]

---

## 3. The Scene (≈ 12–15 min, the same for weeks)

Now let yourself arrive somewhere else. Not tomorrow. Not someday. Here, as if it has already happened.

[pause 8s]

It's the end of a working day. You're at your desk. The light is soft. Everything that needed to be done today… is done.

[pause 10s]

You reach out and close the laptop. Hear the small click.

[pause 8s]

You lean back in your chair. Feel the backrest behind you. Your hands rest on your thighs.

[pause 10s]

And there it is. The quiet. Nothing is pulling at you. Nothing is waiting. Not because you're forcing it away, but because there is simply nothing there to pull.

[pause 12s]

It's handled.

[pause 15s]

Notice where you are. What do you see in front of you? What do you hear in the room? Let the details come on their own.

[pause 20s]

What you create flows. Work comes, work is paid, work goes out into the world. It's ordinary now. So ordinary that you hardly think about it.

[pause 12s]

I am anchored, unbothered and certain.

[pause 20s]

Close the laptop once more. The click. The backrest. The quiet.

[pause 15s]

It's handled.

[pause 20s]

You don't need anyone to tell you. You don't need to check. You know the way you know your own name.

[pause 15s]

I respond. I don't react.

[pause 20s]

Once more, from the beginning. The desk. The soft light. The click. Leaning back. Hands resting. The quiet.

[pause 30s]

It's handled.

[pause 20s]

I create with freedom, creativity and purpose, and it flows with ease.

[pause 30s]

Stay here. You don't have to do anything else. Just stay in this evening, at this desk, in this quiet.

[pause 45s]

---

## 4. Drift (the same every night, voice gets quieter, then ambient only)

You can fall asleep right here.

[pause 15s]

In this room. In this quiet. As the one you already are.

[pause 20s]

Nothing to do.

[pause 20s]

It's handled.

[pause 30s]

*(Ambient sound from here, slowly fading over 5–8 minutes. No outro, no "wake up".)*

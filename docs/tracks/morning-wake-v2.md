## 1. Up

Good morning. You're up. That's the first thing done today, and you did it. Whatever you're about to do now, coffee, water, the bathroom, just do it. You don't have to sit down for this, and you don't have to close your eyes. Let my voice come along while you move. Your hands can be busy. Your head can listen.

Your head might already be running. The list. The messages you haven't read yet. Something from yesterday that didn't get finished. Something that might happen today. That's fine. It's what a head does in the morning. You don't have to fight it, and you don't have to follow it either. For the next few minutes, just follow this voice instead. Every time you notice you've drifted off into the list, that's not a mistake. That's the moment you come back.

Right here. Your feet on the floor. That's where you are.

Let's take one real breath before anything else. In through the nose, slow… all the way down into your belly.

[pause 3s]

And out through the mouth, long, longer than it came in.

[pause 5s]

## 2. The first minutes

Here's something most people never notice. The first minutes of the day don't belong to the day yet. Nothing has happened. No one has asked anything of you. No email has landed, no one has called. The day is still empty. And whatever you put into these first minutes is the tone everything else follows.

Most mornings, the tone gets set by accident. By the phone. By the first worry that shows up. By some old story that wakes up a second before you do and starts talking. Tired again. Behind again. Not enough again. And because it's the first voice of the day, it sounds like the truth. It isn't. It's just early.

This morning, you get to set the tone on purpose. Not by forcing anything. Not by pretending everything is perfect. Just by deciding who is walking into this day, before the day gets the chance to decide it for you.

That's what from the end means. You don't wait for the day to show you who you are. You start as the person you'd be if everything you're working toward were already done. And you let the day catch up.

Right here. Whatever's in your hand right now. Notice it.

---

## 3. Water, coffee, whatever's first

If you're making coffee or getting a glass of water, slow it down by just a few seconds. Notice the sound of it. The cup, the weight of it in your hand. The first sip. The temperature. How it moves down into your body.

This is not a small thing. This is your body waking up, cell by cell. It carried you through the night, kept your heart going, your breath going, without you asking it to. And now you're giving something back to it. Water. Warmth. A few seconds of attention.

Think about how someone moves who lives in a strong, healthy body. Someone who has energy in the morning and doesn't have to fight their way into the day. They're not rushing. They don't drink their coffee like it's an emergency. They stand there for a moment. They take the first sip like it matters. Because it does.

So do that. One sip, and let it be the only thing happening.

[pause 4s]

Right here. That's the whole morning in one sip.

## 4. Your body

While you keep going with whatever you're doing, let your attention move through your body for a moment. Not to check what's wrong with it. Just to say hello.

Your feet. They're holding you up right now. Your legs, the ones that run across the court, that carry you up the stairs. Your back, your shoulders. If your shoulders are pulled up toward your ears, let them drop. Just a little. Your jaw. A lot of people clench their jaw in the morning before they even know they're stressed. Let it go loose. Let your tongue rest. Your forehead, smooth.

This is the body that's going to carry you through this whole day. You don't have to love every part of it right now. Just notice that it's on your side. It always has been.

And here's your first line. It's one of yours. You wrote it about who you are when everything is right. Let me say it, and then you say it after me, out loud or inside, whatever works right now.

I live in vibrant health. Strong, disciplined, nourished and energized.

[pause 8s]

You don't have to feel it all the way yet. Just notice that it's yours. Nobody gave it to you. You chose it.

Right here. Breathe.

## 5. The mirror

At some point this morning, you'll stand in front of a mirror. Brushing your teeth, shaving, washing your face. Most people look at themselves in the morning like an inspector. Checking. Looking for what's off. Tired eyes. A line that wasn't there last year. Whatever it is.

This morning, try looking differently. Look at that face like it belongs to someone you trust. Someone who's been through a lot and is still standing. Someone who keeps getting up. Because that's exactly who it is.

Look at your eyes for a moment. Not at the skin around them. At the eyes. There's someone in there who has been there the whole time. Through every good day and every bad one. Through every time you doubted yourself. That one didn't go anywhere. That one is watching right now.

And that one is not the voice in your head. The voice in your head is the one that talks. The one in your eyes is the one that listens. You can always come back to that one. It never needs a reason.

Right here. In the mirror. That's you.

Now, while your hands keep doing what they're doing, let's look at how someone stands who trusts their own life. Their feet are planted, the weight even on both. Their shoulders are down and a little back, not pulled up, not braced for anything. Their chest is open. Their breath goes low and slow, into the belly, not high and quick in the throat. And their eyes are steady. They look straight ahead, not down, not around for the next problem. Nothing about them is in a hurry.

Let your body borrow a bit of that while you stand there. Feet. Shoulders. Breath. Eyes. Even a little shift is enough. That's how you stand when you know. Keep it.

## 6. From the end

Now let's do something that sounds simple. Let's jump to tonight. The day is already over. You're lying in bed, the sheets cool, your head heavy on the pillow. And somebody close to you asks, so, how was your day? And you hear yourself answer. Good. It was a really good day. And it's not because everything worked. Not because nothing went wrong. You say it because of how you were in it.

And you keep talking, the way you do at night when you're tired and honest. I didn't rush today. When that annoying message came in, I took a breath before I answered. I said what I meant. I said no once, and it was easy. I took a break in the middle of it all, and I didn't feel bad about it.

That's what you hear yourself say tonight. Not what you got done. Who you were while it happened. Listen to your own voice saying it. Calm, a bit slow, nothing to prove. Good day.

Now come back to right now, this morning, and bring that voice with you. You've just heard how today goes, from the inside. Not the details. The way you are in it. That's already decided. Everything else is just the day filling in the details.

That's how it works. You don't get there by fighting through the day. You start from there, and the day follows.

Right here. This breath. You're already in it.

## 7. Trust

Here's something about the mind. It thinks it has to have the whole day figured out before the day even starts. Every meeting planned, every conversation rehearsed, every problem solved in advance. And when it can't do that, it gets loud.

But you don't need to know how today goes. You never have. Every good thing that ever happened to you, you didn't see coming. The people who changed your life showed up without warning. The ideas that mattered came when you weren't trying. That's not luck. That's how life moves. It doesn't follow your plan. It answers who you are.

So you don't need a plan for the next hour right now. You need to trust that when the moment comes, you'll know. A thought shows up, a door opens, a person calls. And you'll recognise it, because you're paying attention instead of worrying.

That brings us to your second line.

I move through life guided by intuition, grounded in trust, and anchored in spirit.

[pause 8s]

Say it once more, inside, slower than before.

[pause 6s]

Right here. Hands. Feet. Breath. That's all you need for the next step.

## 8. What you do

There's one more line, and it's about your work. Not about what you'll get done today. About how you work when you're the person you wrote about.

Someone who creates from freedom doesn't work like they're being chased. They don't sit down at the desk with a tight chest. They don't open their inbox like it's a list of everything they're failing at. They start with what matters. They let ideas come. They enjoy the moment something clicks. And when it's enough for the day, they stop, and they're not afraid that stopping means losing.

That's not someone who works less. That's someone whose work comes from a different place. From creating, not from fear. From purpose, not from proving.

Here's your third line. You wrote it with a number at the end. Leave the number out. It doesn't need it.

I generate money with freedom, creativity and purpose, with ease.

[pause 8s]

Notice which word in that line feels most alive right now. Freedom. Creativity. Purpose. Ease. Just one. That's the word for today.

[pause 5s]

Right here. That word is yours now.

---

## 9. Out the door

You're almost through. Maybe you're dressed by now. Maybe you're still standing there with the coffee. It doesn't matter.

Here's what to take with you. Not a plan. Not a list. Just three things. Your breath, because it's always there, even when the day gets loud. Your word, the one you just picked, because one word is enough to come back. And the way you stood in front of the mirror, because your body remembers that, even when your head forgets.

Sometime today, your head will start running again. That's fine. It will. And when you notice it, you don't have to fix anything. One breath. One word. Shoulders down. And you're back.

Every time you come back, it gets a little easier. Not because you're trying harder. Because you're practising who you are. That's all this is. Practice. Morning by morning, breath by breath.

Take one more breath with me. In, slow and deep…

[pause 3s]

And out, long.

[pause 4s]

The day hasn't happened yet. But you already know how you're in it. Go on. Go and live it from there.

# Padel Track v1: "Before the match" (nerves)

Status: **v1, voiced right away** at Kevin's request. Change anything that feels wrong and it gets re-voiced.
Use: before a padel match, in the car, on the bench or in the changing room. Eyes open or closed. About 6 minutes. Ends awake and ready.
Voice: ElevenLabs "James". `[pause Xs]` = silence added during stitching.
Source for the identity lines: `docs/kevin-2.0.md` (Present, Playful, Self-trusting, Calm power, Breath-led).

Rule check:
- Identity lines: three, each spoken once.
- No promise of winning, no score, no tactics. It's about who walks onto the court, not about the result.
- No claimed feelings: the nerves are named as energy in the body, never denied.

---

## 1. Arrive

Before you go on court, take a few minutes for yourself.

[pause 3s]

Wherever you are, let your feet find the ground. Let your hands rest.

[pause 5s]

---

## 2. Breath

Breathe in through your nose… one more short sip on top… and a long, slow breath out through your mouth.

[pause 8s]

Again. In… sip… and let it all go.

[pause 8s]

Now just let every breath out be a little longer than the breath in.

[pause 15s]

---

## 3. The nerves

Maybe there's something buzzing in you right now. A tight chest. Restless legs. Thoughts running ahead to the first serve.

[pause 6s]

You don't have to get rid of it. That's your body getting ready to play. The same energy that feels like nerves is the energy that makes you quick.

[pause 10s]

Let it be there. Breathe into it. You're not fighting it. You're riding it.

[pause 12s]

---

## 4. From the end

Now jump ahead. The match is over.

[pause 4s]

You're walking off the court. Your shirt is wet. Your legs are warm. You're shaking hands over the net.

[pause 10s]

Somebody says something, and you laugh. Whatever the score was, you know you played free. You went for your shots. You were there for every ball.

[pause 12s]

Notice what that's like. Walking off the court like that. Easy. Loose. Done.

[pause 15s]

That's where you're coming from today.

[pause 8s]

---

## 5. Three lines

I am present. One ball at a time.

[pause 15s]

I am playful and self-trusting.

[pause 15s]

I play with calm power.

[pause 15s]

---

## 6. Go

One word for the court: next ball. Whatever just happened, good or bad, there's only the next ball.

[pause 8s]

Take one more deep breath in… and let it out slowly.

[pause 6s]

Shake out your hands. Bounce a little on your feet.

[pause 5s]

Go play.

[pause 3s]

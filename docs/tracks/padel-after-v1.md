# Padel Track v1: "After the match" (when you want to tear yourself down)

Status: **v1, voiced right away** at Kevin's request. Change anything that feels wrong and it gets re-voiced.
Use: after a match that felt bad, before the inner critic takes over the evening. In the car, on the bench, at home. About 7 minutes. Ends awake.
Voice: ElevenLabs "James". `[pause Xs]` = silence added during stitching.
Source for the identity lines: `docs/kevin-2.0.md` (Whole, Playful, Standard "Chooses himself + peace", Level 1 → Level 100).

Rule check:
- Identity lines: three, each spoken once.
- No promise ("next time you'll win"), no tactics, no analysis of the game. That can come later, from a calm place.
- No claimed feelings: the self-criticism is named as weather, never argued with, never denied.
- The revision is lived from inside ("step into it"), same as the evening revisions.

---

## 1. Stop

The match is over.

[pause 3s]

Before you go over it again, stop for a moment. Nothing about this game needs to be decided right now.

[pause 5s]

Breathe in through your nose… and a long, slow breath out.

[pause 6s]

Once more. In… and let it go.

[pause 8s]

---

## 2. The voice

Maybe there's a voice right now that's going through every mistake. The easy ball you missed. The serve that didn't come. What you should have done.

[pause 8s]

You don't have to argue with it. And you don't have to believe it. It's weather. Loud weather, after a game. It's not who you are.

[pause 12s]

That voice belongs to the old level. The one that turns a game into a verdict about you.

[pause 10s]

You're the one hearing it. Not the one it's talking about.

[pause 12s]

---

## 3. One point

Now let one point come back to you. The one that stings the most.

[pause 8s]

Just see it, like a clip on a screen. There it is.

[pause 6s]

Now let it play again. Same court, same ball. But this time, step into it as the one you already are.

[pause 6s]

The ball goes wide. You breathe out. Maybe you even smile. You turn around, walk back to your spot, and you're already there for the next ball.

[pause 12s]

Nothing to prove. Nothing to make up for. Just the next ball.

[pause 10s]

Let it play one more time, this way. Loose. Light. Already moving on.

[pause 20s]

That's how that point went.

[pause 6s]

---

## 4. Three lines

I am whole, whatever the score.

[pause 15s]

I am playful. It's a game, and I get to play it.

[pause 15s]

I choose myself and my peace.

[pause 15s]

---

## 5. Keep one good thing

Now let one good moment from the match come back. A shot that landed. A laugh with your partner. A rally you were fully in.

[pause 10s]

There it is. Let it be bigger than it was. Stay with it a little longer.

[pause 20s]

That's the one you keep from today.

[pause 8s]

---

## 6. Return

Take one more slow breath in… and a long breath out.

[pause 6s]

Feel your feet. Notice where you are.

[pause 5s]

The game is done. You can let it be done. Go on with your day.

[pause 3s]
